# Widget  (type: 'widget')
Purpose:       A panel that **floats over** the page, pinned to an edge (right, left, bottom or top): an AI assistant, a support chat, quick notes. It does not push the layout. Unlike `Window` (centered, modal, draggable).
Anatomy:       `body > .op-layer` > `.op-widget.op-panel` (`op-widget--right|left|top|bottom`, `is-floating`, `is-open`) with the `Panel` header (title, icon, tools), body and bars. It inherits everything from `Panel`.
Config:        (Panel) + `side: 'right'|'left'|'top'|'bottom'` (right), `floating: true` (`false` = a regular panel in the layout, not floating; `side` does not apply),
               `size` (width on the sides, height on top/bottom; default from the tokens), `sideSwitcher: false` (buttons in the header so the user picks the side) and `sides: ['left','right','bottom']` (which sides the switcher offers),
               `draggable: false` (the user moves the widget by its header: drag with the pointer, or Alt+arrows / Shift+Alt+arrows with the focus on the header; moving it detaches it from the side, and `setSide()`/Alt+Home put it back),
               `resizable: false` (the user resizes it: by the inner edge while docked, by a corner grip once moved; minimum 160 px),
               `closable: true`, `closeAction: 'hide'`, `closeOnEsc: true` (Esc with the focus inside closes), `autoFocus: false` (on open the focus goes to the widget itself; `true` focuses its first field: a phone keyboard pops up), `layout: 'fit'`.
API:           `open()`/`show()`, `close()`/`hide()`, `setOpen(bool?)` (without an argument it toggles; `toggle()` is still the Panel collapse), `isOpen()`, `setSide(side)`, `setSize(size)`, `getSide()`, `getPosition()` (`{x, y}` in window px, or `undefined` while it is on a side), `resetPosition()`, `setResizable(bool)` + the Panel API (`setTitle`, `setIcon`...).
Events:        `before-show` (cancelable), `show`, `before-hide` (cancelable), `hide`, `side-change(side)`, `move({x, y} | undefined)`, `resize({width, height})`, and the Panel ones (`before-close`, `close`).
States:        `is-open` (visible, with a slide transition), `is-floating`.
Keyboard/ARIA: Not modal: the page stays usable and the focus is **not** trapped. `role=complementary` named by its title; the resize handle is a focusable `role=separator` (WAI-ARIA window splitter) with `aria-orientation` and `aria-valuenow` (px): arrows resize by 16 px, Shift+arrows by 64; Esc closes when the focus is inside; the focus goes back to the opener. The side switcher is buttons with `aria-pressed`.
Tokens:        `--op-widget-width` (24rem), `--op-widget-height` (26rem), `--op-widget-wide` (width on top/bottom), `--op-widget-offset` (distance from the edge), `--op-widget-shadow`, `--op-widget-radius`, `--op-widget-bg`, `--op-widget-grip` (the edge handle's thickness), `--op-widget-corner` (the corner grip).
Sandbox cases: each side, the side switcher, `floating: false` (docked in the layout), with a `Chat` inside, a custom `size`, several widgets at once, `draggable` with the side switcher, `resizable` (docked and moved); `workbench` demo: widgets that are not chats (notes, a focus timer, view settings) over a grid.
Out of scope:  remembering the position and size between visits (the app stores them on `move` and `resize`), a floating launcher button, remembering the chosen side (the app stores it on `side-change`).
