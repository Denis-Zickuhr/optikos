# Grouping  (type: 'grouping')
Purpose:        Row grouping with a header, a count and totals per group, as a Grid plugin (the Ext `grouping` + `summary` features).
Anatomy:        The grid's rows get virtual header rows (`op-grid__row--group`: `op-grid__twist`, `__group-name`, `__group-count`) and total rows (`op-grid__row--total`, `is-group-total` / `is-grand-total`) between the records; the records of a group carry `is-grouped`. Headers and totals are rows of the virtualized body like any other.
Config:         `grouping({ field | by, label?, summary?, collapsed? })`: `field` is the record field to group by, or `by(rec)` any function (a year, a range); `label(key, recs)` is the header text (default the key); `summary: 'group' | 'grand' | 'both'` (group) says where the total rows go; `collapsed` starts with every group closed; `controls: true | { expand?, collapse?, by? }` puts icon buttons inside the grid, in a row with the pager (expand all, collapse all and, when `choices` is given, a "Group by" menu with a "No grouping" entry), each one left out with `false`; `choices: [{ text, field | by }]` are the ways to group that menu offers. On the columns: `summary: 'sum' | 'avg' | 'min' | 'max' | 'count' | (values, recs) => value` (your own operation: gets the column's values and the records); on dates only `min` (earliest), `max` (latest) and `count` apply, since a date has no sum; and `summaryRenderer(value, col)`; a total uses the column's `format`. A total row is named by `aria-label="Total"`; the word is shown in the first column when that column has no summary of its own.
API:            `expandAll()`, `collapseAll()`, `toggle(key, open?)`, `setGroupBy(field | fn | null)`, `setControlVisible(control, visible)` and `setControlDisabled(control, disabled)` (`'expand' | 'collapse' | 'by'`) on the plugin; `summarize(column, records)` is exported for your own use. The Grid gets `setRowLayout(rows)`, `getRowEntry(i)` and `getRowCount()`, which any plugin can use (see `RowEntry`).
Events:         On the Grid: `group-toggle(key, open)`.
States:         Group open or closed (`aria-expanded` on the header row). Sorting orders the rows inside each group (groups keep the order of their first record). A remote store groups what it loaded (the current page), not the whole set: the server must sort by the group field to keep groups whole across pages.
Keyboard/ARIA:  On a focused header: Enter / Space toggle, → opens, ← closes (↑/↓ move through headers, records and totals as usual). Headers are `role=row` with `aria-level=1` and `aria-expanded`, records `aria-level=2`; the grid stays `role=grid` (a group is not a tree: the arrow keys do not move between levels). Headers and totals cannot be selected.
Tokens:         `--op-grid-group-bg`, `--op-grid-total-bg`, `--op-grid-indent`.
Sandbox cases:  `data`: grouped by a field with a count and sum, grand total, a grouping function, collapsed.
Out of scope:   Several levels of grouping, drag a column to a group bar, a server-side group query, a group summary in the header row.
