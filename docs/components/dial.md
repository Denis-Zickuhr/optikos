# Dial  (type: 'dial')
Purpose:       Picks one value from a range (an age, a year) or a list (the months) on a flat ruler that slides under a fixed needle, with a large readout whose digits roll like an odometer; `shape: 'circle'` turns it into a clock face.
Anatomy:       `.op-dial` (Field) > `.op-dial__readout` (the value: each digit is a `.op-dial__digit` > `.op-dial__reel`, other characters are plain text) and `.op-dial__face[role=slider]` (focusable) > `.op-dial__tape` > `.op-dial__tick` (`.is-major` on snap points, with a `.op-dial__tick-label`), plus the fixed `.op-dial__needle`. With `shape: 'circle'` (`.op-dial--circle`) the ticks go around the face, the needle becomes the clock hand, turning on a hub at the center.
Config:        (Field) + a range: `min: 0`, `max: 100`, `step: 1`; or a list: `options: ['Jan', …] | [{ value, label }]` (the value is the item's value). `snap` (snap points): for a range every multiple of N (default `10`, so decades fall on 1990, 2000…), for a list every N items (default `1`), or an array of values, or `false`.
               `shape: 'linear'` (`'linear' | 'circle'`), `format: (value) => string` (readout and labels), `labels: 'snap'` (`'snap' | 'all' | 'none'`: which ticks are labelled), `value` (default: `min` / the first item).
API:           (Field) `getValue/setValue` (the dial animates to it), `setMin(n)`, `setMax(n)`, `setStep(n)`, `setOptions(list)`, `setSnap(snap)`, `setShape(shape)`, `setFormat(fn)`, `next(n = 1)`/`prev(n = 1)` (steps), `nextSnap()`/`prevSnap()`. The value always stays in the range and on a step (or on an item).
Events:        `change(value)` while dragging (once per value crossed) and on every key or click, `slide-start`, `slide-end(value)` when the dial comes to rest; with `debounce`, `settled(value)`; plus the Field ones (`focus`, `blur`, `invalid`...).
States:        `is-dragging`, `is-gliding` (coming to rest after a flick), `is-disabled`, `is-readonly`. Motion: a slow drag rests on the nearest step; a flick glides with inertia and rests on the snap point nearest to where it would stop, among those ahead; the readout digits roll and a text value slides in; the ruler resists past its ends. All of it is off with `motion.disable()` or reduced motion.
Keyboard/ARIA: WAI-ARIA APG "Slider" pattern. The face is `role=slider` with `aria-valuemin/max/now/valuetext` and `aria-orientation=horizontal` (a list uses the item's position for `valuenow` and its label for `valuetext`). ←/↓ and →/↑ ± one step; Page Up/Down go to the next/previous snap point; Home/End to the limits. A click on a tick or its label goes there.
Tokens:        `--op-dial-spacing` (distance between ticks on the ruler), `--op-dial-item-spacing` (the same for a list), `--op-dial-height` (ruler), `--op-dial-size` (circle), `--op-dial-hub`, `--op-dial-tick-fg`, `--op-dial-major-fg`, `--op-dial-needle`, `--op-dial-readout-size`, `--op-dial-readout-fg`, `--op-dial-roll-duration`.
Sandbox cases: age (0–120, decades), birth year (1920–2026), months (a list), months as a clock (`circle`), a minutes clock (0–59, snap 5), `format` (`v => v + ' years'`), disabled and read-only, inside a Form.
Out of scope:  two values (a range selection: use Slider), a vertical ruler, more than a few thousand positions (every tick is rendered), free typing.
