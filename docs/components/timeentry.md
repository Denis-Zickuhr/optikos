# TimeEntry  (plugin: `timeEntry()`)
Purpose:       Time tracking on a `range` Timeline: create, move, resize and remove entries by pointer or keyboard, with the total per lane. Lanes can be days, people or resources.
Anatomy:       The timeline gets `op-timeline--entry`. Lanes get `op-timeentry__lane` (`is-under|met|over` against `target`); each editable bar gets two `op-timeentry__handle` (`--start`, `--end`); while dragging, `op-timeentry__ghost` (a new entry) and `is-dragging|is-resizing|is-invalid` on the bar; `op-timeentry__cursor` (the keyboard position on a lane) and `op-timeentry__grand` (the grand total) at the end.
Value:         The timeline's items (`{ title, lane, start, end, ... }`), or the records of its `store`. Times are written as the timeline reads them: `'HH:mm'` on a one-day axis, ISO otherwise.
Config:        `timeEntry({ step?, minDuration?, duration?, overlap?, moveAcrossLanes?, target?, editable?, defaults? })` in the timeline's `plugins`: `step` minutes everything snaps to (15); `minDuration` shortest entry in minutes (`step`); `duration` length of an entry made with the keyboard (60); `overlap: false` keeps two entries of a lane apart: a drag stops at the neighbour, a move is refused (true); `moveAcrossLanes` lets an entry change lane (false); `target` minutes a lane should add up to; `editable(item)` returning `false` locks an entry (approved, invoiced); `defaults` are the fields of a new entry besides `lane`, `start` and `end`; `form(item, 'create' | 'edit')` opens your own dialog (a Window with a Form, a `MessageBox.prompt`...) and resolves with the fields to set (`title`, `text`...) or `false` to cancel: on create the entry only exists once it answers, on edit (double click, Enter) the fields land as a `change`. Also exports `formatDuration(minutes)` (`7h30`).
API:           —; it works on the timeline (`setItems`, `getItems`).
Events:        On the timeline: `before-create({ lane, start, end })` → `create(item)`; `before-change(item, next, old)` → `change(item, next, old)`; `before-remove(item)` → `remove(item)` (the `before-*` ones can be canceled by returning `false`); `edit(item)` on double click or Enter. With a `store` the records are updated here (the app saves); without, the `items` are.
States:        Locked (`is-locked`, `aria-disabled`), invalid while dragging (`is-invalid`: overlap or too short), dragging, resizing.
Keyboard/ARIA: On a bar (horizontal timeline; on a `direction: 'vertical'` one ↑/↓ are time and ←/→ are lanes): ←/→ move by a step, Shift+←/→ move the end, Alt+Shift+←/→ move the start, ↑/↓ change lane (`moveAcrossLanes`), Enter `edit`, Delete/Backspace remove, Esc cancels a drag. On a lane (a tab stop): ←/→ move the cursor, Enter adds an entry there. Every change is announced in a polite live region; a refused one says why. Touch: a bar drags at once, an empty lane needs a long press (about half a second) so a swipe still scrolls.
Tokens:        `--op-timeentry-handle`, `--op-timeentry-cursor`, `--op-timeentry-grip`; colours come from the timeline and the theme.
Sandbox cases: `data`: a week of days with `target` 8h, overlap forbidden, a locked entry, lanes as people with `moveAcrossLanes`.
Out of scope:  Recurring entries, zoom levels, dragging between two timelines, an edit form (the app answers `edit`), saving (the app answers `change`).
